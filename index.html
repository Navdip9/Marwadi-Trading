About Marwadi Trading

Your Trusted Trading Partner

Marwadi Trading is a Rajkot-based trading company engaged in the supply of premium products including Dry Curry Leaves, Mint Leaves, Tomato, Ginger, Garlic, Green Chilli, and other fresh commodities.

We bridge the gap between local farmers and wholesale buyers, ensuring that quality products reach the market at competitive prices while maintaining the highest standards of freshness and authenticity.

Fresh from Farm

Direct sourcing from local farmers ensures the freshest products reach your doorstep.
